# Test claims by expected risk score

Generated by `python -m eval.make_risk_files` (fictional people, hospitals and policies).
Upload `claim/` files to the Claim form and `policy/` files to the Policy form.

**Each set is single use.** ClaimLens remembers every invoice number it has seen (across all accounts), so uploading the same files a second time adds critical *duplicate invoice* findings and the score jumps to 100. To test again, regenerate a fresh set first.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| score-100 | 100 | high | retype_total | editing_tool (high), font_outlier (critical), hidden_text (critical), sum_mismatch (high) |
| score-70 | 70 | high | late_edit, admit_after_discharge | date_order (high), inpatient_outside_stay (high/high), late_edit (medium) |
| score-50 | 50 | review | late_edit, admit_after_discharge | date_order (high), inpatient_outside_stay (high), late_edit (medium) |
| score-30 | 30 | review | editor_resave, incremental_save | editing_tool (high), multiple_saves (medium) |

Points: critical 40, high 20, medium 10, low 5, coverage 0; capped at 100. Bands: under 30 low, 30–59 review, 60+ high.
